\section{AI-Use Declaration}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{AI-Use Declaration}

This document was written with assistance from Claude Code (Anthropic CLI) with the API Key offered by Kimi/Moonshot. In keeping with the course's view of AI as an accountable collaborator rather than a black box, this declaration clarifies how the AI was utilized specifically for producing this written report, distinct from the product's development. Specifically:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Content Extraction and Synthesis}: The synchronously updated \\ \text{AI\_Tools\_Usage\_Review.md}and other development files were provided to the AI as a "history book". The AI was utilized to extract and synthesize key operational steps, technical decisions, and project milestones from these logs to construct the narrative framework of this document.
    \item \textbf{Human Theoretical Leadership}: The human author took the exclusive lead on all scientific and theoretical foundations. The mathematical modeling, the core algorithm development, the precise tuning of reinforcement learning parameters, and the design of the numerical experiments were conducted entirely by the human. There was virtually no AI participation in establishing these critical theoretical constructs.
    \item \textbf{Formatting and Drafting}: The AI assisted in formatting the LaTeX document, generating the initial structural scaffolding, and refining the academic prose based on detailed human outlines.
\end{itemize}

The human author takes full responsibility for the document's arguments, evidence, and conclusions.

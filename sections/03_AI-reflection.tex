\section{Building with AI}\label{sec:building}

\subsection{Human Agency as the Driving Force of Creation}

Throughout the development of Maverick-SORT, we adhered to a core principle of historical materialism: \textbf{human intellect and agency remain the fundamental driving forces of creation}. AI is a revolutionary means of production, but it is not the architect. 

The core mathematical foundation of the system---the dynamic scheduling model, the Markov Decision Process (MDP) formulation, and the Knowledge-Guided Deep Reinforcement Learning (KGDRL) architecture---was conceived, constructed, and validated entirely by the human author based on real-world pharmaceutical logistics pain points. The AI (Claude Code) did not invent the algorithm; its role was strictly confined to execution, translating human-defined theoretical models into PyTorch boilerplate, and generating the Streamlit UI scaffolding. 

The division of labor evolved into a strict ``Human-Led Direction, AI-Led Implementation'' paradigm, as detailed below:

\subsection{A Concrete Example: Systems Thinking vs. Local Patching}

One instructive failure occurred during the Streamlit Cloud deployment, perfectly illustrating the limitation of the AI's local reasoning compared to human systems-level thinking. 

The AI had generated \texttt{hf\_integration/config.py} with a straightforward \texttt{import transformers} at module load time. On Streamlit Cloud, it triggered a catastrophic cascade:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \texttt{transformers} scans all internal model registries on import.
    \item Image models attempt to import \texttt{torchvision}.
    \item \texttt{torchvision} is absent on the Cloud environment $\rightarrow$ \texttt{ModuleNotFoundError} floods the logs.
    \item Streamlit's background file watcher detects perceived module changes and restarts the app.
    \item The restart repeats the import $\rightarrow$ resulting in an infinite crash loop.
\end{enumerate}

When asked to fix this, the AI proposed adding \texttt{torchvision==0.22.0} to the requirements. However, this required \texttt{torch==2.7.0}, creating an unresolvable conflict with our pinned \texttt{torch==2.12.0}. The AI then blindly proposed bumping the \texttt{torch} version, which would have destroyed our CUDA compatibility. 

\textbf{Human judgment}: The AI was trying to satisfy a bad dependency, treating the symptom rather than the disease. As recorded in our development logs, the human recognized the root cause and implemented a three-layered architectural defense: (1) injecting \texttt{TRANSFORMERS\_NO\_ADVISORY\_WARNINGS=1} to suppress the scan; (2) moving the import into a lazy-loading function; and crucially, (3) disabling Streamlit's file watcher entirely via \texttt{.streamlit/config.toml}\parencite{Jurowetzki2026}. The AI lacks the environmental awareness to understand that background watchers do more harm than good in complex cloud dependency scenarios; that operational wisdom must come from the human.

\subsection{Defensive Programming: Knowing When AI Output is Naive}

Our criterion for keeping AI-generated code went beyond mere functional correctness. We evaluated its structural soundness against the chaos of real-world operations. 

A primary takeaway from our development review was that \textbf{AI intrinsically assumes a "happy path."} When generating the data-upload module for the warehouse dashboard, the AI wrote code assuming the user's uploaded CSV would perfectly match our expected schema. 

The human operator, understanding the unpredictable nature of actual warehouse data, had to force a paradigm shift: from \textit{``assume the schema is correct''} to \textit{``assume the schema is wrong.''} We manually intervened to rewrite the data ingestion layer using defensive programming patterns (e.g., \texttt{df.columns.intersection(expected)}), ensuring that Maverick-SORT would gracefully handle missing columns or malformed pharmaceutical datasets without crashing. The AI can write the code to process the data, but only the human understands the hostility of the operational environment it will be deployed in.
